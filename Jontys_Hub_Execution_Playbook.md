### Margin-Safe Customer Referral Engine
Operating at `< 25%` gross margin means blanket discounts are strictly forbidden. The referral program is engineered around **minimum basket thresholds** to protect net profit:

* **The Offer:** *"Invite a Friend — Both Enjoy ₹500 Privilege"*
* **The Rules:**
  1. The new friend gets **₹500 OFF** on their first purchase of **₹5,000 or more** (minimum 2.5 items).
  2. Upon the friend's purchase, the original customer receives a **₹500 VIP Credit** redeemable on their next purchase of **₹5,000 or more**.
* **Financial Verification (Margin Safety Check):**
  * Average Bill: ₹5,000
  * Gross Margin (25%): ₹1,250 gross profit
  * Less Referral Privilege: -₹500
  * **Net Gross Profit Earned:** **+₹750** on a customer acquired at **ZERO paid advertising cost**!
  * Result: Both customers feel rewarded, average order value stays above ₹5,000, and gross profit remains solidly protected.
