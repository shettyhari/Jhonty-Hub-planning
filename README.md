## Step-by-Step Narrative Flow (14 Slides)

1. **Slide 01 – Cover:** Luxury brand positioning & store anchor (`B.N. Reddy Nagar, LB Nagar`).
2. **Slide 02 – Step 01 · Brand Foundation:** Five menswear categories (`Casual Shirts`, `Formal Shirts`, `T-Shirts`, `Jeans`, `Trousers`) and store benchmarks (`₹2,000` avg price, `₹5,000` avg bill, `< 25%` gross margin).
3. **Slide 03 – Step 02 · Consumer Shift:** Why modern men discover via video, respond to fabric/fit education, and trust specialist boutiques.
4. **Slide 04 – Step 03 · Strategic Position:** Core foundation today paired with 4 focused growth unlocks.
5. **Slide 05 – Step 04 · Buyer Profiles:** Four distinct male customer segments with staple categories and video hooks.
6. **Slide 06 – Step 05 · Local Catchment:** Radial expansion from B.N. Reddy Nagar, Vanasthalipuram & Hastinapuram to LB Nagar, Chintalkunta & Kothapet.
7. **Slide 07 – Step 06 · 5-Stage Funnel:** Video Discovery → Educate & Position → High-Intent Filter → Store Visit Goal → Retention.
8. **Slide 08 – Step 07 · Video-View Remarketing:** Pooling 50%+ video watchers, educating on value before price, establishing luxury store authority, and driving high-intent store visits.
9. **Slide 09 – Step 08 · B2C Channel Architecture:** Instagram Ads, Facebook Ads, YouTube Ads, Google Display Ads, Google Business Profile & WhatsApp Community (daily/weekly drops & referral perks).
10. **Slide 10 – Step 09 · Lookbook & Creative Direction:** Visual storytelling concepts across all 5 menswear lines.
11. **Slide 11 – Step 10 · Margin-Safe Basket Growth:** Scaling ₹5,000+ outfits without blanket discounting under a `< 25%` margin guardrail.
12. **Slide 12 – Step 11 · 90-Day Execution Plan:** Sequential 4-phase rollout from video capture to community-led referral scale.
13. **Slide 13 – Step 12 · Measurement Loop:** Tracking Video Viewers (50%+), Educated Walk-Ins, and **Marketing-Generated Gross Profit vs. Ad Spend**.
14. **Slide 14 – Closing · Partnership Next Steps:** `Understand → Build → Grow`.
